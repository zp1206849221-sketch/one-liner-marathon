## The joke

> My fitness tracker says I burned 2,314 calories, climbed 40 flights of stairs, and swam half a mile yesterday, which is remarkable, because I spent the entire day on the couch. The tracker was on the cat.

## Why it works

The first sentence stacks three escalating, oddly precise achievements
(calories, stairs, then an outright swim) against a full day on the couch,
so the reader is primed for an explanation that cannot exist. The second
sentence is the whole twist in five words: the hero of the story was the
cat, and the joke ends on that single word.
